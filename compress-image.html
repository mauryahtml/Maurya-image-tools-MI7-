Compress one or multiple images without losing much quality.

Select one or more images to start.

📖 How to Use

  1. Tap Select Multiple Images and choose one or more images.
  2. Each image will appear separately with its preview and image details.
  3. Enter the required target size in KB.
  4. Tap Compress Image.
  5. The compressed result will appear inside the same image card.
  6. Use Download to save the compressed image.
  7. Use Share to share the compressed image from your device.

⭐ Features

  • Compress single and multiple images
  • Reduce image size in KB
  • Supports JPG, PNG and WEBP
  • Separate preview for every image
  • Separate compression result for every image
  • Download images individually
  • Share images individually
  • Image dimensions shown in pixels
  • Processing happens in your browser
  • Images are not uploaded to our server

🔒 Privacy

Your images are processed directly in your browser. They are not uploaded to or permanently stored on the MI7 server by this image compression tool.

After leaving or refreshing the page, temporary image data used by the browser is cleared.
