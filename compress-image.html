📖 How to Use

1️⃣ Click Select Image(s).

2️⃣ Select one or multiple images.

3️⃣ Enter Target Size (KB or MB).

4️⃣ Click Compress.

5️⃣ Wait for compression to complete.

6️⃣ Preview compressed image(s).

7️⃣ Click Download.

8️⃣ Download single image or ZIP.

9️⃣ All processing happens inside your browser.
